# Concorrentes do Dojo do Foco: focus/study timer apps (state as of Nov 2026)

Note on sources: most usage and revenue numbers come from app-store listings (developer-written), third-party estimators (Sensor Tower, Similarweb, Noctral) or SEO review sites, several written by competitors. Treat them as rough. Two primary pages (Fast Company, CBC) returned HTTP 403 and could only be read through search snippets. Search budget was limited (about 17 calls), so some apps (Session, Focus Keeper, Pomofocus, Fokus, Focus Plant) are thinly covered. See Gaps.

## 1. Per-app core loop, signature feature, monetization, size

### Takeaway
The winners pair one simple emotional loop (a creature or tree that "suffers" if you leave, and a home or forest that grows) with phone-level enforcement (app blocking, Live Activity). They monetize with cheap subscriptions or cosmetics, never with ads in the focus moment. Social study (YPT, StudyStream, Study Together, Focusmate) is a separate, large category built on body doubling and leaderboards.

### Cited Findings

**Focus Friend (Hank Green / Bria Sullivan, 2025)**
- Loop: a bean knits while you focus. If you disturb it before the timer ends, it drops its stitches. — [CBC via search snippet](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/productivity-apps-doomscrolling-1.7627301)
- Rewards are socks (free) and scarves (Pro), traded for furniture and decorations for the bean's rooms (kitchen, living room). The listing promises "hundreds of decorations". Bean skins are sold separately at $2.99 to $5.99. — [FOCO review, Oct 2026 (competitor)](https://www.tryfoco.com/focus-friend-app-review/)
- Features: sessions up to 120 min, Deep Focus Mode that blocks apps through iOS Screen Time, a lock-screen Live Activity timer, Pomodoro breaks, background music that keeps playing when the app is closed, stats and achievements, and a bean collection (including Hank and John Green beans). — [focusfriend.me (may be an unofficial fan or affiliate site; the official domain appears to be yourfocusfriend.com)](https://focusfriend.me/); [FOCO](https://www.tryfoco.com/focus-friend-app-review/)
- Pricing: free core. Pro costs $3.99/month, $19.99/year or $39.99 lifetime (US, Oct 2026). Older articles say $1.99/month or $14.99/year. No ads and no data collection are claimed. — [FOCO](https://www.tryfoco.com/focus-friend-app-review/); [focusfriend.me](https://focusfriend.me/)
- Ratings: 4.8 from 9,368 ratings (US App Store). Google Play: 4.9 from 12.2K reviews, 1M+ downloads. — [FOCO](https://www.tryfoco.com/focus-friend-app-review/)
- Criticisms: no task list, step breakdown or calendar; decorating during breaks can become a hyperfocus time sink; English only; conflicts with other Screen Time apps (Opal, Forest). — [FOCO](https://www.tryfoco.com/focus-friend-app-review/)
- No in-app social features are described. "Body doubling" is framed as the bean's presence. — [focusfriend.me](https://focusfriend.me/)

**Forest (Seekrtech)**
- Loop: plant a virtual tree that grows while you stay off the phone. Coins can be spent on real trees through Trees for the Future. The company counter shows about 2.1M trees planted (Sep 2026, self-reported). Real-tree planting is premium and capped at 5 per account. — [Make Headway review](https://makeheadway.com/blog/forest-app-review/)
- Size: Google Play shows 10M+; other claims range from 40M+ (teardown) to 60M and 100M+ (reviews). These conflict. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cc.forestapp&hl=en_AU); [NextLeap teardown PDF](https://assets.nextleap.app/submissions/ForestAppProductTeardown-35023f48-5d6a-4dd4-a5a8-01df65382a6a.pdf); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_(application))
- Monetization: Wikipedia says that in Dec 2025 Seekrtech moved to a free download with a Forest Plus subscription (about $5.99/month per a review). It was historically a paid iOS app. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_(application)); [Makerstack](https://makerstack.co/reviews/forest-review/)

**Flora**
- Loop: like Forest, a tree dies if you leave. It adds an optional, self-reported cash stake ("Flora Price"), blocks apps, and partners with Trees for the Future and Eden Reforestation (84,500+ trees claimed). One free version with small purchases and no ads. — [Gridfiti](https://gridfiti.com/apps-like-flora/); [focusdog.app comparison](https://focusdog.app/magazine/best-focus-pet-apps/)

**Finch: Self-Care Pet**
- A bird pet grows as you complete self-care goals. Free, with an optional Finch Plus subscription. Bootstrapped, with no venture money. — [Review Rounds](https://review-rounds.com/finch-business-model-bootstrapped-self-care-pet-app/)
- Size (modeled estimates): Sensor Tower US, Q2 2025: about $380K monthly revenue and 126K downloads. A Jan 2026 snapshot gives about $2M/month revenue and 400K downloads/month. The estimates conflict. — [Sensor Tower blog](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-health%20and%20fitness-units-us-600af518241bc16eb8dce802); [apprank.io](https://apprank.io/finch-self-care-pet)

**Study Bunny**
- One coin per 10 min of study buys items and music for the bunny. Also has a to-do list, flashcards and a study tracker. Free with ads; no blocking mentioned on the store page. — [habitrewards.me](https://habitrewards.me/blog/apps-like-study-bunny.html); [iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/products/study-bunny/alternatives/)

**Habitica**
- Your whole to-do list (habits, dailies, to-dos) is an RPG. Parties of up to 30 fight bosses together, and your missed Dailies damage the whole party. — [habitbox review](https://habitbox.app/blog/habitica-review)
- Core is free. The subscription is $4.99/month or $47.99/year and adds gems, drops and cosmetics. Reports say finding a party now requires knowing people already, and there are complaints of quest bugs (competitor blog, weak source). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habitica-gamified-taskmanager/id994882113); [habi.app](https://habi.app/insights/habitica-alternatives/)

**LifeUp (Android; iOS as "ulives")**
- Self-designed reward shop: rewards can be real-life (a break, a purchase), rest time, or in-app stats, bought with coins from tasks. Also loot boxes, crafting ("key + locked chest"), custom currencies, user-set XP and coin per task, widgets, many statistics and a "feelings" log. — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/lifeup/about); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/lifeup)
- Paid once, with no subscription or ads; there is also a Lite version. — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/lifeup/about); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/net.sarasarasa.lifeup.free)

**Focus To-Do**
- Pomodoro plus a task list. Premium is about $1.99/month or $11.99 lifetime and adds cross-device sync; free data stays local. — [goalsandprogress](https://goalsandprogress.com/focus-to-do-review-2026/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/focus-to-do-pomodoro-tasks/id1258530160)

**Pomofocus**
- A free web Pomodoro timer built around tasks, with reports, templates and integrations; has ads. — [usefoci](https://usefoci.com/blog/best-focus-apps-for-students-2026); [mindfulsuite](https://www.mindfulsuite.com/reviews/best-focus-timer-apps)

**Focusmate**
- One-on-one live video co-working ("body doubling") with a stranger, in sessions of 25, 50 or 75 min. Free tier is 3 sessions/week. Plus costs about $8/month billed yearly (some sources say $12 monthly); figures conflict. — [flat.social](https://flat.social/guides/focusmate-review); [lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/focusmate-pricing)

**Tiimo**
- Visual, color-coded day planner for neurodivergent users. AI breaks tasks into steps and ranks them, and it adds live widgets, a focus timer and AI-guided reflections. It won Apple's iPhone App of the Year 2025, after being an Apple Design Awards 2024 inclusivity finalist. iOS, iPad, Watch and web. Premium is about $10 to $12/month or $54/year (sources conflict). — [Daring Fireball](https://daringfireball.net/2025/12/2025_app_store_award_winners); [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/apple-intelligence/articles/apples-iphone-app-ai-tool-123900823.html)

**Structured**
- Visual timeline day planner for iOS, Mac and Watch with a redesigned focus timer. Claims 1 to 1.5M users. Free, with Pro at $2.99/month, $29.99/year or $99.99 lifetime (US listing). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1499198946)

**Brain.fm**
- "Functional music" for focus, backed by company-funded research (Northeastern MIND Lab, 2024). Independent replication is lacking, and the "119%" marketing claim is not in the cited study. $14.99/month or $99.99/year (Sep 2026). — [theunhacked](https://theunhacked.com/brain-fm-review-what-the-science-actually-says-and-who-should-skip-it/); [outliyr](https://outliyr.com/brainfm-review)

**YPT (Yeolpumta, Korea)**
- A study stopwatch with study groups (see friends' live status) and real-time rankings within the same exam category (e.g. CSAT, medical exam). The developer claims 5M+ users. Users report bugs in time recording. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pallo.passiontimerscoped); [iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/products/ypt-yeolpumta/)

**StudyStream / Study Together**
- StudyStream (YC S21) runs free 24/7 Zoom focus rooms with up to 1,000 students; it has 194K to 400K community members by various counts. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2213744)
- Study Together is a non-profit Discord server with about 1.05M members, calling itself the largest study server. — [Discord listing](https://discord.com/servers/study-together-595999872222756885)

**Duolingo streak mechanics**
- Letting learners equip up to 2 Streak Freezes increased daily active learners by +0.38%. Duolingo calls this small but meaningful at scale. — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)
- 10M+ users have 365+ day streaks; DAU passed 50M. — [TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/duolingo-engagement-over-monetization-ai-language)
- Duolingo ran a limited-time "restore lost streaks" campaign to win back lapsed users. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/duolingo-is-restoring-lost-streaks-for-a-limited-time-aiming-to-recover-lapsed-users/)

**Small cozy pixel web timers (direct visual competitors of Dojo)**
- Pixeldoro: a free pixel-art Pomodoro world with lofi radio, pets, a room to decorate and studying "with friends". — [pixeldoro.io](https://pixeldoro.io/)
- FocusPixel (browser): timer, lofi, pets and XP. — [focuspixel.app](https://www.focuspixel.app/)
- Pomofox: a pixel fox runs laps while you focus and naps on breaks. — [pomofox.com](https://www.pomofox.com/)
- PixFocus: a Chrome extension where coins unlock visual timers. — [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pixfocus-gamified-cozy-vi/hdndbhnidlhphocggjbhdpeeaiehmadh)

### Inferences
- Dojo already covers most of LifeUp's reward-shop idea and Habitica's RPG layer, plus things nobody listed has: real weather and time of day, plan-before and review-after, and Obsidian saving. Its gaps are distribution and enforcement (phone, blocking, Live Activity) and social presence, not the depth of the game.
- The closest direct threats are small pixel web timers (Pixeldoro, FocusPixel), which already add "study with friends" rooms. Dojo's differentiation is its Japanese theme, Portuguese language, ADHD focus and the yōkai metaphor. Portuguese matters because Focus Friend is English only.

### Gaps
- No reliable data found for Session (Mac), Focus Keeper, Fokus, Focus Plant or the Lofi Girl channel (subscriber count not retrieved). Lofi Girl was not researched beyond the search query.
- Pomofocus traffic numbers were not found.
- Habitica's current user count was not found.

## 2. Cozy, pixel-art and gamified apps that launched or grew in 2024–2026, and why

### Takeaway
Focus Friend (soft launch late July 2025) is the standout: No. 1 on the US App Store above ChatGPT, then Google Play recognition. It grew on creator reach, an "anti-app" message against doomscrolling, and a single cute, guilt-light mechanic. Tiimo (iPhone App of the Year 2025) shows the ADHD visual-planning angle winning platform recognition.

### Cited Findings
- Focus Friend reached No. 1 on the App Store in the US, Australia, Canada, Ireland and New Zealand, with about 740K downloads shortly after its late-July 2025 soft launch. — [Fast Company (snippet)](https://www.fastcompany.com/91388304/focus-friend-app-store-hank-green-bria-sullivan)
- Focus Friend passed ChatGPT for the top App Store spot and reached more than 1M downloads. CBC framed it within a "cure for scrolling" trend, with experts warning that more tech can add stress. — [CBC (snippet)](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/productivity-apps-doomscrolling-1.7627301)
- Focus Friend became No. 1 overall on both the App Store and Google Play; DFTBA sells merch for it. — [DFTBA store](https://store.dftba.com/collections/focus-friend)
- Its site claims "Google Play App of the Year" and "Best for Personal Growth" without naming a year, and quotes Hank Green: "The anti-app is the top app". It also claims positive motivation rather than shame, simplicity, and flexible rather than hard blocks. — [focusfriend.me](https://focusfriend.me/)
- The product is still being updated: outfits were added to the shop on Oct 21, 2026. — [FOCO](https://www.tryfoco.com/focus-friend-app-review/)
- Tiimo won iPhone App of the Year 2025 for AI task breakdown and visual, neuro-inclusive planning. — [Daring Fireball](https://daringfireball.net/2025/12/2025_app_store_award_winners)
- Finch's revenue grew from about $380K/month (Q2 2025) to an estimated $1 to 2M/month (Jan 2026, modeled). — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-health%20and%20fitness-units-us-600af518241bc16eb8dce802); [apprank.io](https://apprank.io/finch-self-care-pet)

### Inferences
- The pattern is a cute companion whose emotional state reflects your focus, plus home decoration as the long-term sink, plus gentle (not punitive) loss. Dojo's pixel character fighting yōkai already fits. Making the character's emotion more visible (e.g. reacting when the user leaves the tab) could reproduce the "don't let the bean drop its stitches" hook without penalties.
- Creator-led distribution (Hank Green's audience) was decisive; the mechanic alone does not explain the virality.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable launch data for Pixeldoro, FocusPixel or Pomofox, and no reason found for their growth.
- The Focus Friend Google Play award year is not confirmed by a primary source.

## 3. Features that recur across successful apps and that Dojo lacks

### Takeaway
The recurring features Dojo lacks are app or site blocking, Live Activity and lock-screen timers, widgets, a mobile presence, social co-presence (rooms, friends, live status, category leaderboards), streak protection (freezes or restores), cross-device sync, and a real-world impact tie-in (trees). Calendar integration and Apple Watch appear mainly in the planner apps (Tiimo, Structured).

### Cited Findings
- App blocking: Focus Friend (Screen Time), Flora and Forest all have it. — [FOCO](https://www.tryfoco.com/focus-friend-app-review/); [Gridfiti](https://gridfiti.com/apps-like-flora/)
- Live Activity / lock screen: Focus Friend. Live widgets: Tiimo. Widgets: LifeUp. — [focusfriend.me](https://focusfriend.me/); [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/apple-intelligence/articles/apples-iphone-app-ai-tool-123900823.html); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/lifeup/about)
- Social: YPT groups with live status and category rankings; StudyStream rooms of 1,000; Study Together's 1M-member Discord; Focusmate 1:1 video; Habitica parties; Pixeldoro "with friends". — sources in section 1
- Streak freezes: Duolingo's +0.38% DAU effect. — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)
- Sync is a paid upsell in Focus To-Do. — [goalsandprogress](https://goalsandprogress.com/focus-to-do-review-2026/)
- Real trees: Forest (about 2.1M) and Flora (84.5K+). — [Make Headway](https://makeheadway.com/blog/forest-app-review/); [Gridfiti](https://gridfiti.com/apps-like-flora/)
- Apple Watch: Tiimo and Structured. — [Daring Fireball](https://daringfireball.net/2025/12/2025_app_store_award_winners); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1499198946)

### Inferences (concrete ideas for Dojo, all doable without accounts)
1. **"Pergaminho de proteção" (streak freeze)**: a weekly or monthly protection for the daily streak, earned with ryō. It fits the "no penalties" rule, and Duolingo's data supports it.
2. **Tab-leave reaction**: use the Page Visibility API so the character "loses concentration" or a yōkai advances when the user switches tabs. Keep it gentle, as in Focus Friend. Optionally offer a site-block list through a small browser extension (blocking cannot be done from a web page).
3. **PWA plus notifications**: an installable PWA gives a home-screen icon and a timer notification, a cheap answer to the missing mobile app, widgets and Live Activity.
4. **Asynchronous or anonymous co-presence without accounts**: a "dojo coletivo" counter ("N pessoas estudando agora"), or a shared room through a link and a simple realtime backend. YPT's category rankings suggest themed rooms (vestibular, ENEM, concurso). This is body doubling for ADHD in the style of Focusmate and StudyStream.
5. **Home or dojo decoration as the long-term ryō sink**: Focus Friend, Study Bunny and Pixeldoro all use this. Watch out for the break-time hyperfocus criticism (limit decoration to after the review, or cap it).
6. **Real-world impact**: Forest and Flora use trees. A Japanese equivalent could be planting a sakura in a "jardim coletivo", or linking to a real NGO later. Optional.
7. **Statistics view**: weekly and monthly heatmaps and time per task or subject (Focus To-Do, YPT, LifeUp). Dojo has a daily summary but may lack longer-range stats.
8. **Export or calendar (.ics)**: export planned or completed sessions. The Obsidian integration already covers part of this.
9. **Loot-box-style random rewards from the user's own shop** (as in LifeUp), with care for ADHD over-stimulation.

### Gaps
- I didn't verify whether Pomofocus, Session or Focus Keeper offer site blocking or widgets.
- No usage data found on how much social features improve retention in focus apps specifically.

## 4. What the most successful apps deliberately avoid

### Takeaway
Leaders avoid shame and harsh punishment, ads during focus, data collection and complexity. The ADHD and cozy apps avoid making the game itself a distraction. The main risk in this category is "more tech adds stress".

### Cited Findings
- Focus Friend claims positive motivation instead of shame or guilt, simplicity over the complex systems of other pet apps, flexible blocks instead of hard ones, no ads and no data collection. — [focusfriend.me](https://focusfriend.me/)
- Flora's cash stake is opt-in and self-reported, and nothing is billed automatically. — [Gridfiti](https://gridfiti.com/apps-like-flora/); [focusdog.app](https://focusdog.app/magazine/best-focus-pet-apps/)
- LifeUp avoids subscriptions and ads (one-time purchase). — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/lifeup/about)
- Duolingo found freezes did not cause people to skip more days, so it softened streak loss rather than making it harsher, and even ran streak restores. — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/duolingo-is-restoring-lost-streaks-for-a-limited-time-aiming-to-recover-lapsed-users/)
- Things to avoid that critics flag: decoration as a break-time sink, and team punishment (Habitica's party damage) that can feel bad. Habitica also shows how complex gamification breeds bugs, as reported for its quests. — [FOCO](https://www.tryfoco.com/focus-friend-app-review/); [habitbox](https://habitbox.app/blog/habitica-review); [habi.app](https://habi.app/insights/habitica-alternatives/)
- Experts warn that focus apps can add stress (CBC). — [CBC (snippet)](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/productivity-apps-doomscrolling-1.7627301)
- Unverified efficacy claims draw criticism: Brain.fm's "119%" claim is not in the cited study. — [theunhacked](https://theunhacked.com/brain-fm-review-what-the-science-actually-says-and-who-should-skip-it/)

### Inferences
- Dojo's "no penalties" principle matches the market leaders, so keep it. Any social feature should avoid public shaming leaderboards, or make them opt-in and cooperative (e.g. the whole dojo fights a weekly yōkai boss together, Habitica-style but without damage from missed days).
- Avoid "neuro" claims for the generated lo-fi music. Keep Dojo free and without ads, which is a selling point against Study Bunny and Pomofocus.
- Keep the 12 classes, 11 realms and so on behind progressive disclosure, so the "one action at a time" principle survives as the system grows.

### Gaps
- I found no founder interviews (Seekrtech, Finch, Focus Friend) stating explicit "we chose not to build X" decisions; the Fast Company article was inaccessible (403).
